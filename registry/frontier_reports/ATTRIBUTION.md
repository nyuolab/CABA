# Attribution

`epoch_notable_ai_models_2026-10-04.csv` is a snapshot of Epoch AI's Notable AI Models database, downloaded on 4 October 2026 from https://epoch.ai/data/notable-ai-models. It is redistributed unchanged under its licence, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0).

Epoch AI, "Data on Notable AI Models". Published online at epoch.ai. Retrieved 4 October 2026 from https://epoch.ai/data/notable-ai-models.

The other files in this folder are CABA's own records:

- `frontier_reports_selected.csv`: the models chosen by the selection rule.
- `frontier_reports_documents.csv`: the documents those models link to.
- `frontier_reports_fetch_log.json` and `frontier_reports_browser.jsonl`: how each document was retrieved, with its length and a checksum.
- `frontier_benchmarks.csv`: the health benchmarks reported in each document, with the reporting passage.

These files are CC BY 4.0. The quoted passages are short excerpts of the cited reports, kept to show where each benchmark is reported. The reports themselves are not redistributed.
