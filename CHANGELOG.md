# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

The version described in the paper.

- 58 benchmarks: 175 were named by 13 evaluation suites and by the frontier model reports selected from Epoch AI's Notable AI Models, 102 were excluded before download and 15 after download.
- RCQ-Map labels (GPT-5.6 sol, reasoning effort none, the paper's guidelines) for 10,659 of the 10,699 sampled items. The other 40, all from Med-HALT, failed the schema's consistency rules.
- Snapshots: suite components on 30 September 2026, Epoch AI Notable AI Models on 4 October 2026.

Later versions will add benchmarks proposed through the issue template, with the same protocol and a new snapshot date. Version 1.0 stays available unchanged.
